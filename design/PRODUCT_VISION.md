# Little Hero Product Vision

## Vision
Build the best after-school companion for children.

## Core idea
Children should feel they are growing a magical world instead of completing chores.

## Experience pillars
1. Story first.
2. Positive reinforcement.
3. Parent-configurable.
4. Beautiful enough that children want to reopen it every day.

## Success metric
The child opens the app voluntarily after school without reminders.
